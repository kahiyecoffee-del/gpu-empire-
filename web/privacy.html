Privacy Policy

AI Overlord: GPU Tycoon ("the game") is developed by Sozer. This policy explains what data the game uses. Last updated: October 2, 2026.

Data we collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Your game progress is saved only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and approximate location to show ads and measure their performance. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first (Google's consent form); you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings › Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We never see your payment details. We only receive a confirmation of which item was bought so we can deliver it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion

Because your progress is stored only on your device, uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. For advertising data, see Google's controls above.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
